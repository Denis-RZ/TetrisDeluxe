How it works

Two modes, two decisions

Classic

Play manually, place pieces, clear lines and chase a better score. Turn BOT on for autoplay practice or demonstration; turn it off to restore manual control.

Tactical Campaign

Play through ten stages. Compare your score with the bot, respect the move limit and reach the goal. The opponent bot is always part of the mode; BOT controls whether your side is manual or automatic.

Controls

  • Tap — rotate
  • Swipe left/right — move
  • Swipe down — soft drop
  • Drop button or swipe up — hard drop
  • Hold — save a piece for later
  • Pause — stop safely during a session
